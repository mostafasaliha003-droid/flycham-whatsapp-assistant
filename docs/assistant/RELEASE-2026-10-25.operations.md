# Conversation and operations release — 2026-10-25

Version: `2026-10-25.operations.1`.

## Customer conversation

The existing 35-second quiet window and Cline-first consultant remain active. The model receives a compact selection from 100 reviewed conversation situations. Application facts, answered-question history and actual job state remain authoritative. Approved greeting, waiting, thanks, goodbye and price-objection variants avoid repetitive wording without inventing a running search, discount or supplier result. Gemma remains a fallback for explicit Cline quota exhaustion only.

An unspecified current/next-month search asks the customer to choose a part of the month. Exact dates, explicit ranges and an explicitly requested whole month bypass that clarification. The selected part is retained across short follow-ups.

FlyCham is probed first, then Air Arabia proceeds under the existing bounded head-start policy. Verified prices are sent in a single completed comparison, with actual airports, dates, flight numbers, departure/arrival times, baggage, final price and Dubai check time. Large comparisons keep every verified option in a durable bounded snapshot: “المزيد” returns the next message without starting another search. References and price facts remain unchanged; only Meta-accepted pages become selectable. Ambiguous delivery is never blindly repeated. Cached pages expire after six hours and cannot survive an unrelated itinerary amendment.

Supplier negatives require explicit validated evidence. Authentication, timeout and unverified outcomes never become “no flights” or “sold out”. Alternate airports and nearby dates are identified explicitly. Existing margins and future FlyCham voucher rules are unchanged.

## Staff operations

`/operations` provides the original REMAL logo, responsive Arabic RTL layout, searchable inbox, actual inbound/outbound messages and receipts, private notes, saved offers, handoffs and metrics. The admin key stays in page memory. Protected requests use Bearer authentication; no credentials are stored in browser storage.

Staff claim durably pauses automated responses for that customer and cancels outstanding quote work. Staff sending requires claim and an actual inbound message within 24 hours, rechecked immediately before dispatch after any queue wait. A body-bound idempotency key prevents duplicate sends. Release returns control to Ahmed. No customer message is sent merely by opening the dashboard.

The encrypted journal retains up to 30 days, with bounded customer/message/text limits. Previously retained history is imported once, at most 24 messages per customer, with explicit approximate timestamps. Legacy history never opens a WhatsApp sending window. Privacy deletion removes the journal as well as assistant state and queued deliveries.

## Portal monitor and fare catalog

The combined worker publishes actual stages and sanitized route/date metadata. A short-lived JPEG preview is allowed only from the trusted active search/results frame. Editable controls, nested documents and identifier-like text are masked. Login, OTP, passenger and payment pages are withheld. Images are held in memory only, expire after 30 seconds, and are returned only through admin-authenticated endpoints when the provider queue is also ready. A failed monitor POST cannot change or delay quote pricing. Render process health alone is not proof of an authenticated supplier session.

The background catalog rotates current/next-month routes, configured airports and both directions; return examples use 7/14 nights and state their dates. Customer work across either provider preempts background catalog work. Fifteen-minute refresh eligibility is not a promise that all routes or dates have been scanned every 15 minutes. Exact quote seeds require a check within 15 minutes. Broader indicative cards may use observations up to six hours old, explicitly state check time and require rechecking. No claim of a complete monthly minimum is made.

## Deliberately blocked capability

Live verified pricing still supports one adult. Adult+child and adult+infant catalog fixtures, party/age-separated keys and read-only capability diagnostics are prepared, but family pricing is disabled. Air Arabia passenger/ancillary handling and FlyCham aggregate-party totals/age flows require supplier evidence before activation. No environment toggle or model output can bypass that gate; a one-adult price is never multiplied into a confirmed family quote.
